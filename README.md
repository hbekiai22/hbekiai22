## What I'm building

### 💳 Pay2gether — group checkout for travel and hospitality
A payment option that villas, hotels, boat charters and venues add to their checkout. Each person in a group pays their own share by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, and the business is paid once the group is complete.

- Public website in English and Greek, group checkout, QR-code pay-at-the-table
- Business dashboard, booking pages, owner admin console
- REST API with webhooks for partner integrations
- Stripe payments with card holds, automatic refunds and deadline handling

`TypeScript` `React` `Node.js (Fastify)` `PostgreSQL` `Stripe API` `Docker` `GitHub Actions`

### 🎟️ MyEventReservation — nightlife table and event reservations
Guests discover events and reserve tables or tickets by choosing their exact table on an interactive venue floor plan. QR-code tickets in a digital wallet, and a dashboard for venues to manage bookings and check in guests.

`Lovable` `React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS`
